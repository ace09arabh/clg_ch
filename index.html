About Us

Marwari College, Ranchi is a reputable institution established in 1963, offering undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Arts, Commerce, Science, and Business Administration. The college is NAAC accredited and affiliated with Ranchi University. It has a strong faculty with experienced professors and a decent placement record, with companies like Tech Mahindra visiting the campus.

Marwari College Ranchi is one of the premier educational institutions affiliated with Ranchi University. The college is committed to providing quality education and fostering academic excellence among students.

We offer a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses such as BCA, BBA, B.Sc IT, MBA, and MCA. Our aim is to develop skilled professionals who can contribute to society and the nation.

The college provides modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a positive learning environment to ensure the overall development of students. We believe in discipline, dedication, and innovation, helping students achieve their career goals.
